Three Things We Learned About Cold Brew

published: 14 September 2026

We spent the summer testing cold brew recipes on our regulars, and the results were not what we expected when we started.
Here is what surprised us most.

In no particular order:

  • Grind size matters more than steep time
  • Filtered water changes the flavour completely
  • Room temperature brewing is faster but less forgiving

Warning: never leave a batch steeping for longer than 24 hours.

Cold brew is not a recipe, it is a habit. You get better at it the way you get better at anything, which is by doing it
badly for a while first.

Priya Raman, The Slow Extraction, Roast Quarterly
